Selected work

Most recent first. Each entry leads with the measurement that mattered, and what it was measured against.

  • Jul – Aug 2026 · Research intern, SEI-LAB, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

    Quadruped Fall Recovery →

    The policy was fine. The robot wasn’t the robot it trained against — 216 g heavier, at 77.6% of the torque-to-weight it had learned on.

    1.4 – 2.1 s
    supine to back on its feet, in 10 of 14 logged recoveries — the other four stalled at 5.4–10.5 s

  • Jan – May 2026 · Undergraduate thesis, Dalian University of Technology, carried out at UC Irvine

    SLAM Walking Robot →

    Two motors, six bars, and no IMU — a walking robot that still maps an indoor lab and knows where it is inside it.

    0.211 m
    AMCL localization RMSE on the robot, against a spec of ≤ 0.25 m over N = 35 readings

  • Oct 2025 – Mar 2026 · Senior design capstone, MAE 151A/B, UC Irvine · five-person team

    ArmY — Grasp-Pose Selection →

    Grasp detection offers a hundred ways to pick up a jar. Choosing one is the whole job — and detecting the target accurately turns out not to be the same thing as placing the gripper accurately.

    7 / 10
    grasp poses within ±1 cm — the requirement the system did not meet, alongside 15/15 target detection

  • Spring 2025 · MAE 106 Mechanical Systems Laboratory, UC Irvine · seven-person team

    Compressed-Air Line-Following Car →

    Five liters of air at two bar is the entire energy budget — no drive motor, a piston engine I designed, and a lane the camera keeps losing.

    62.3 m
    travelled on one fill, 86% of the 72.67 m the compressed air should theoretically give

  • 2024 season · RoboMaster, Dalian University of Technology · Engineer robot

    RoboMaster Engineer — SCARA Arm →

    The arm never lifts its own shoulder motors, the beam is a carbon octagon chosen for stiffness per gram, and the lift is two lifts — coarse and fast, then fine and precise.

    80%
    of the inertia a joint accelerates is the motor's own mass, if you mount it on the joint — so neither shoulder motor rides on the arm
